Our Craftsmanship

Five Steps to Perfection
01

Formulation

Premium fragrance oils are carefully selected and blended to achieve the desired scent profile, ensuring the perfect balance of notes and longevity.

🧪
02

24-Hour Maceration

The freshly blended perfume rests for a full day, allowing the alcohol and fragrance oils to integrate into a uniform, harmonious composition.

⏱️
03

8-Week Maturation

The perfume matures under controlled conditions for eight weeks, developing better balance, richer character, smoother transitions, and improved dry down.

🌙
04

Quality Inspection

Every batch is meticulously visually inspected before bottling to ensure consistent quality and perfection across all CUPIEL fragrances.

✓
05

Packaging & Delivery

Each perfume is packed under the CUPIEL quality system and carefully prepared for retail, ready to reach discerning customers worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Extrait de Parfum (also called Pure Perfume) is the highest concentration of fragrance oils in alcohol, typically 15-40%. This means superior longevity, rich scent, and exceptional quality compared to other fragrance types.

The 8-week maturation process allows fragrance oils and alcohol to integrate perfectly, developing better balance, richer character, smoother transitions between notes, and improved dry down. This creates a more refined, memorable scent experience.

Due to high fragrance oil concentration, CUPIEL perfumes typically last 8-12 hours on the skin. Application on moisturized skin and pulse points can extend longevity even further.

CUPIEL offers four sizes: 10mL Pocket Edition (travel), 20mL Standard Edition, 50mL Premium Edition, and 100mL Luxury Edition. Choose based on your usage and preference.

Store CUPIEL fragrances in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Always keep the bottle tightly closed after use. Proper storage ensures longevity and optimal fragrance performance.

Yes! Our Custom Series allows you to design a personalized perfume based on fragrance family, intensity, and preferences. Contact us for a consultation with our fragrance specialists.

We offer standard and express shipping across India. International shipping is available for select regions. Fragrances are carefully packaged to ensure safe delivery.

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your CUPIEL fragrance, contact our customer service for returns or exchanges.
