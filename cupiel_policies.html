Shipping Information

Shipping Methods

We offer the following shipping options across India:

  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days | Free above ₹500
  • Express Shipping: 2-3 business days | Additional charges apply
  • Overnight Shipping: Next business day | Available for select locations

International Shipping

We currently ship to select countries. International orders may take 10-15 business days depending on customs clearance and local delivery partners.

Shipping Address Requirements

  • Complete street address
  • City, state, and pincode
  • Valid phone number
  • Name of recipient

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. Use this to monitor your package status.

Shipping Delays

While we strive to meet promised delivery dates, delays can occur due to factors beyond our control (weather, customs, carrier issues). We are not liable for indirect damages resulting from shipping delays.

Packaging

CUPIEL fragrances are carefully packaged to ensure safe delivery. The packaging itself is designed to preserve fragrance quality and present a premium unboxing experience.

Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your package arrives damaged or is lost:

  • Contact us within 48 hours with photos
  • File a claim with the shipping partner
  • We will replace or refund after claim resolution

Returns & Refunds Policy

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We're confident you'll love CUPIEL fragrances. If you're not completely satisfied, we offer a 30-day return window from the date of delivery.

Return Eligibility

Products must meet the following conditions:

  • Within 30 days of delivery
  • Unopened and in original condition
  • With original packaging and seals intact
  • With proof of purchase (order confirmation)

Return Process

  1. Contact customer support: returns@cupielperfumes.com
  2. Provide order number and reason for return
  3. Receive return shipping instructions
  4. Ship product back to us (prepaid label provided)
  5. Receive refund within 7-10 business days of receipt

Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened or partially used fragrances
  • Custom or personalized orders
  • Items outside the 30-day window
  • Products without original packaging
  • Items purchased from third-party sellers

Refund Timeline

  • Initial review: 2-3 business days
  • Return shipment: 5-7 business days
  • Refund processing: 7-10 business days after receipt
  • Total timeline: 2-3 weeks

Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, refunds may take an additional 3-5 business days to appear in your account.

Exchanges

If you'd like to exchange for a different fragrance instead of a refund, we can process exchanges within the 30-day period with no additional shipping charges.

Defective Products

If you receive a defective or damaged product:

  • Contact us immediately with photos
  • We'll provide a replacement or full refund
  • Return shipping is covered by us

Final Sale Items

Certain clearance or limited-edition items may be marked as "Final Sale" and are not eligible for returns or refunds.
