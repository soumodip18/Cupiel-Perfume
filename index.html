Our Story

Every memorable journey begins with curiosity. What started as an exploration into fragrance composition gradually evolved into a pursuit of craftsmanship. Inspired by globally admired perfume profiles and driven by a desire to create accessible premium fragrances, CUPIEL Perfumes was established to deliver refined scent experiences without compromising on quality. Each formulation is developed through careful experimentation, structured blending, and continuous refinement. Instead of simply creating fragrances, we focus on building personalities—scents that become part of everyday confidence, celebrations, achievements, and memories. As a brand within the Mul Biotech Farms Ecosystem, CUPIEL reflects the same philosophy that drives the ecosystem: combining scientific thinking, disciplined product development, and long-term value creation. While the ecosystem is known for innovation across biotechnology, regenerative agriculture, and sustainable solutions, CUPIEL represents its commitment to creating premium consumer products through research-led craftsmanship. Every bottle carries not only a fragrance, but also the passion for learning, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

Why CUPIEL

Every CUPIEL fragrance is crafted using high-concentration Extrait de Parfum formulations and premium fragrance oils. Our production follows a carefully designed quality process including 24-hour maceration and an 8-week maturation cycle, allowing every fragrance to develop improved balance, smoother transitions, and greater depth before reaching the customer. Rather than chasing trends, we focus on creating timeless fragrances that express personality, confidence, and elegance.

How to Apply

  • Apply on neck and wrists.
  • Spray behind the ears and inner elbows.
  • Do not rub after application.
  • Store in a cool and dry place away from direct sunlight.
